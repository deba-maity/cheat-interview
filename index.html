Providers & fallbacks

Connect once on this computer: choose Get API key, sign into your provider account, create a key, paste it below, and Save settings. Groq supports both answers and listening. A copied app does not carry another computer's saved keys.

The app reloads your saved keys at launch. You can also keep your own .env beside the portable executable; it is read at every launch and overrides saved keys for the matching provider. It is not included in the build. New keys must be created in your account; the app cannot generate them.

Lightweight screen capture is the default. Enable Detailed screen only for small text; local OCR is loaded on demand and released after 15 seconds idle. Hide, minimize, or lock the computer to stop capture and pending work.
